Effective date: August 30, 2026

1. Overview

Hashmark is a Markdown editor for iPhone and iPad. Your documents are stored on your device by default. Hashmark does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or developer-controlled server, and it does not track you.

2. Documents and local data

Documents, drafts, recovery copies, app preferences, AI provider settings, and API keys are stored inside Hashmark’s app container on your device. File modification dates are used locally to sort and display recent activity. Hashmark does not send this information to the developer.

You can delete documents in Hashmark or the Files app. Deleting Hashmark removes data stored only in its local app container, subject to device backups managed by you and Apple.

3. Optional iCloud Documents

iCloud sync is off by default. If you enable it, your documents are transferred to Hashmark’s iCloud Drive container and processed by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Hashmark has no separate copy on a developer server.

Turning off sync first downloads a local copy and does not delete the cloud copy. You can manage or delete the cloud copy in the Files app and your iCloud settings.

4. Optional AI features

AI features are optional and use your own API key. Hashmark currently supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Moonshot Kimi, and Zhipu GLM. You may also configure a custom endpoint for the selected provider’s native API format.

Before the first connection to a provider and endpoint, Hashmark shows the recipient and asks for permission. If you allow it, Hashmark may send the following directly to that provider or custom endpoint to perform the action you requested:

  • Your API key and selected model information.
  • Your prompt, selected text, document context, and follow-up answers.
  • Images, camera photos, PDFs, text files, or Hashmark documents that you explicitly attach.
  • Search queries when you enable provider-native web search.

These requests do not pass through a Hashmark developer server. The selected provider or custom endpoint may associate requests with your account and retain or use data under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not send information you are not authorized to share. For custom endpoints, you are responsible for evaluating the endpoint operator.

You can withdraw permission for the current provider and endpoint in Settings → AI Endpoint → Data Sharing. Withdrawal stops future requests but cannot delete information already processed by the provider. Contact that provider or endpoint operator to exercise rights over data it holds.

5. Camera, photos, and files

Camera, photo, and file access occurs only after you choose the corresponding action. Selected content stays on the device unless you explicitly attach it to an AI request, share it using the system share sheet, save an exported image, or enable iCloud as described above.

6. Tracking, analytics, and advertising

Hashmark does not use advertising identifiers, tracking technologies, third-party analytics SDKs, or developer-operated crash reporting. Apple may provide TestFlight and App Store diagnostics under Apple’s policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app. iCloud content remains until you delete it from iCloud. Hashmark attempts to release temporary provider file references created for an AI request, but provider-side retention is controlled by the provider or custom endpoint. Hashmark’s developer cannot directly retrieve or delete data held by Apple, an AI provider, or a custom endpoint.

8. Children

Hashmark is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-operated service. Users should not send a child’s personal information to an AI provider without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when Hashmark’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello1024lc@gmail.com.
